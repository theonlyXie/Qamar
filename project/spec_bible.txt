PART I

Experience soul

The user is the adventurer. Qamar is the intelligent guide who keeps the route alive when real life changes.

1. The experience promise

A traditional diet feels like a map that becomes useless after one wrong turn. Qamar should feel like a guide that notices the turn, understands what happened, redraws the remaining route and stays beside the user. The emotional outcome is continuity: I did not fail; the route changed, and I still know what to do next.

MMORPG metaphor: Borrow the useful structure of an MMORPG fairy guide—presence, orientation, quests, progress and a shared journey—without borrowing childish art, fantasy jargon in serious moments, cluttered HUDs, random rewards or dependency mechanics.

4. Canonical character direction

Figure 2. Canonical Orb concept: use its materiality and state language; do not add a face.

The Orb must feel as if it occupies the same world as the interface: light refracts through it, its veil carries momentum, its tail loses energy, and its halo responds to state. It is not a sticker placed above the UI. Production animation should preserve continuity of volume and light across every state.

8.1 The Orb and branches are one organism

The constellation is not a menu drawn around a character. Its branches are extensions of Qamar's lunar body: when the Orb breathes, a nearly invisible luminance moves through the closest filaments; when it becomes attentive, nearby twigs orient toward the active decision; when navigation opens, moonlight travels from the core to each destination. This shared energy origin is what makes the system feel alive rather than decorated.

Element

Relationship rule

Core → branch

Every branch begins inside the Orb's halo; no disconnected line may appear before the originating light reaches it.

Branch → node

A destination node receives and reflects the beam. It does not glow independently until the branch arrives.

Breath

Resting branches carry only a 4-8% luminance tide synchronized loosely—not mechanically—with the Orb's breath.

Focus

One branch gains width, speed and lunar-white core; siblings lower contrast but remain discoverable.

Completion

A single return wave travels node → Orb, then settles. No fireworks, vine growth or endless shimmer.

Interruption

Light pauses in place and fades to a stable resting state; it never snaps, breaks or withers.

11. Constellation behavior

The five destinations are always complete: Today, Log, Plan, Progress and You. There are no inactive future-agent nodes.

The constellation blooms into available empty space. From a right-edge Orb it arcs left; from a left-edge Orb it arcs right. RTL does not reverse the meaning of spatial safety.

Node orbit radius is 104-152dp depending on viewport. Maintain at least 48dp targets and 12dp separation at 200% text.

Open in 220ms; labels reveal at 50ms stagger; selected ray gains violet-to-cyan light and one haptic tick; transition begins on release.

Dragging outside the constellation cancels with no route change. Returning to the center opens chat only after release and explicit highlight.

A simple vertical destination sheet replaces the constellation for screen readers, Switch Control, large text overflow or when Reduce Motion requests it.

11.2 Branch geometry and material

Use one cubic spine per destination with one to three short secondary twigs. Curves are asymmetrical and organic, but destination geometry stays stable enough to learn.

The branch is layered: 1-2dp lunar-white core, 3-6dp violet/cyan energy, and an 8-18dp blurred aura depending on focus. Never render a hard neon cable.

Light always travels outward from the Orb for opening/commit and inward for completion/cancel. Reversing this grammar without a state reason weakens the character's physical logic.

Maximum visible motes: two per branch, eight across the full tree. Motes follow the spine; random background sparkles do not communicate navigation.

Branches route around text, camera controls, prices and destructive actions. If safe space is insufficient, use the accessible destination sheet rather than compressing the tree into clutter.

Avoid literal roots, vascular veins, lightning, fire, spiderwebs, cables and biological growth. The material is emitted moonlight with gentle living intelligence.

14.1 Su Points phoenix identity

Figure 8. The rising phoenix is the only Su Points currency mark; its meaning is renewal and continued progress.

The phoenix turns Su Points from a generic reward counter into a meaningful symbol of Qamar's product promise: the user can return, recover and continue. It is a currency emblem—not a second character. It never speaks, reacts, flies through the interface or competes with the Orb.

Identity field

Design contract

Meaning

Renewal after interruption, earned progress and continuity. Never wealth, speculation, firepower or status dominance.

Master silhouette

One symmetrical rising phoenix with lifted crescent-like wings and tapered tail. Abstract head; no eyes or facial expression.

Currency token

Deep midnight-blue circular medallion; moon-gold/amber phoenix; optional pale lunar highlight and restrained violet rim.

Small mark

At 16-24dp use a simplified filled silhouette with no feather lines, crescent or outer text. It must survive grayscale and 200% zoom.

Standard mark

At 32-48dp show three primary feather groups per wing, central body and two-part tail; one enclosing ring maximum.

Hero medallion

At 64dp+ allow embossed depth, lunar crescent/halo and subtle enamel texture. No words or denomination inside the token.

Color

Phoenix uses Warm Sand #F6C97A and Warning Gold #FBBF24 as material colors, not warning semantics; highlights may use Moon Glow #E9ECFF.

Motion

One 350-500ms wing-light glint on credit; one downward dim on debit; no flapping, flame, spin, bounce, shower or slot-machine count.

Placement

Prefix wallet balances, quest rewards, spend prices and ledger amounts. Use once per numeric group; never repeat beside every word 'Su Points'.

Semantic label

Announce the numeric amount and 'Su Points'; the phoenix image is decorative when the adjacent text already names the currency.

14.3 Phoenix misuse

Do not replace Qamar's crescent, the Orb, application icon or Cipher Labs mark with the phoenix.

Do not use an Egyptian eagle, heraldic double-headed bird, realistic animal illustration, cute mascot, flame silhouette, casino chip or cryptocurrency coin.

Do not put 'Su', 'SP', a denomination or circular text inside the emblem. The amount and the words Su Points remain adjacent interface copy.

Do not recolor the phoenix red for errors, green for credit or gray for insufficient funds. Status comes from text, signs and surrounding UI; the currency identity stays stable.

Do not animate wings, fire or flight. A material light glint is the maximum movement because the phoenix is an emblem, not a second living guide.

Do not show multiple phoenix coins raining, stacking or spinning. One mark beside one balance/reward group is enough.

PART V

Core experience journeys

Each journey is a continuous exchange with the same guide.

18. First launch: awakening, not onboarding theatre

A thin crescent appears; within 1.2 seconds it becomes the resting Orb. Continue is immediately available—animation never holds the user hostage.

Qamar states the promise and AI/non-medical boundary, creates a guest identity, resolves 18+ eligibility and consent, then asks only the minimum questions needed for a target.

Each answer physically changes the guide's attention, not its affection. The sequence ends with a usable first route before sign-in, notification or payment pressure.

19. Daily loop: guide, act, adapt

Today opens on the next decision, not a score. The Orb occupies intentional empty space and guides once toward the highest-value action.

The user logs what happened. Qamar analyzes, asks for confirmation, writes only the confirmed record, updates the remaining day and offers one action.

If the day changed, Recovery redraws unconsumed meals. No restart screen, lost streak or compensatory fasting/exercise language appears.

20. Meal logging: capture without false certainty

Hold the Orb, sweep to Log, then choose photo, voice, text, barcode, recent or manual. The same methods remain available in an accessible list.

The living branches bloom from the Orb into capture choices, then retract before camera or microphone use. The Orb docks during capture, works visibly during analysis, and becomes a calm witness during confirmation.

The result shows what changed and the next decision. A phoenix-marked Su Points credit may acknowledge a useful behavior, but never replace nutrition clarity or trigger a reward shower.

21. Planning and recovery: the map redraws

The plan is a route through the day, not a rigid prescription. The Orb travels the route while the user scrolls and exposes Why for each meal.

Substitutions compare realistic alternatives. Recovery changes only the remaining day and refuses punitive compensation.

The chosen change animates as a path reroute, then settles; the animation is descriptive, not magical certainty.

22. Progress, Journey and Su Points

Progress presents consistency, logged behavior, useful trends and optional weight—not a total-body score.

Journey turns lifetime useful behavior into Levels 1-20 and controlled Orb/moonbeam-tree cosmetics. Branch maturity is expressed through richer lunar flow and more graceful structure—not more clutter. Spending points never lowers lifetime progression.

The phoenix-branded wallet separates available balance from lifetime earned, shows every ledger entry and exact redemption price, and states no cash value, transfer or expiry at launch. The phoenix symbolizes renewal; it never becomes a second assistant.

23. Trust, billing and safety

On source, privacy, consent, checkout, delete and unsupported states, Qamar becomes still. Information—not character performance—takes visual priority.

The Orb remains a visible navigation portal. Leaving a transactional or destructive flow requires a plain confirmation only when work may be lost.

No fantasy terminology appears in renewal disclosure, nutrition evidence, uncertainty, clinical boundaries or data rights.

36. Orb implementation contract

Concern

Build requirement

Renderer

Rive state machine preferred for interactive vector/mesh states; use deterministic state inputs, not free-running decorative timelines.

State inputs

route, role, activity, attention, network, calmMode, reduceMotion, edge, keyboard, capture, transaction, safety context, treeOpen, focusedNode and branchPhase.

Hit testing

Visual may be 48-64dp; semantic hit area &gt;=56dp and separated from destructive/payment controls.

Physics

Critically damped snap; no bouncy toy easing. Tail follows velocity with a capped length and decays after settle.

Branch renderer

Use stable cubic spines plus layered glow/core/motes. Branch geometry is deterministic per edge/size class; only light phase changes continuously.

Performance

60fps target; degrade branch motes, aura blur and secondary twigs before frame rate. Core navigation remains responsive under AI/network load.

Persistence

Store normalized edge + vertical fraction by portrait/landscape/size class. Never sync exact coordinates across devices.

Semantics

Arabic label 'قمر'; actions: talk, open destinations, move left/right, reset position; state announced only when meaningful.

Phoenix assets

One vector master with hero, standard and micro simplifications; do not rely on raster downscaling for the wallet chip.

Analytics

orb_tap, orb_hold, orb_drag, constellation_open, branch_bloom, node_focus, node_select, cancel and accessible_action—without conversation content.

37. Acceptance checklist

No screen contains an application bottom navigation bar or duplicate persistent tab shell.

A visible, semantic Orb portal remains on every usable route, including camera, keyboard, checkout and confirmation contexts.

Every global destination is reachable with hold+sweep, an accessible tap list, screen-reader actions and supported voice routing.

Tap, hold and drag thresholds do not conflict; accidental navigation rate is tested with left- and right-handed users.

Route changes preserve drafts, scroll, source screen and transaction state or warn before abandoning them.

The Orb has no eyes, mouth, cartoon face, pet needs, emotional coercion or endless attention loops.

Every animation has a semantic equivalent and a Reduced Motion variant; Calm Mode removes reward pressure without removing utility.

Every branch begins inside the Orb halo, carries purposeful outward/inward light, avoids critical content, and has Available, Focus, Commit, Cancel, Offline, Calm and Reduced Motion states.

Arabic RTL, mixed Arabic/English/Arabizi, 200% text, VoiceOver/TalkBack and Switch Control are validated across S01-S62.

Nutrition estimates remain editable and confidence/source-labelled; safety, evidence, privacy and billing use literal language.

No Health OS, sleep, HRV, hydration, movement or workout metrics appear in the nutrition MVP.

Every Su Points surface uses the approved rising-phoenix emblem at the correct simplification level; no 'Su' coin, generic star, fire animation, casino styling or alternate currency icon remains.

Su Points show available and lifetime values separately; redemption is explicit, deterministic, reversible on failure and never pay-to-win.

Frame-time, battery, thermal and memory tests prove the living Orb does not damage core usability on target founder devices.

39. Final design principle

The standard: If the user remembers a bar, a dashboard or a mascot, the experience is ordinary. If the user remembers that Qamar stayed present, understood where they were, and quietly showed the next path, the experience is working.
